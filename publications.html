Dissertation

PhD Dissertation • In Progress

Hybrid System Dynamics-Agent Based Model for Health-Related Quality of Life in Adolescent and Young Adult (AYA) Cancer Patients and Survivors

Joseph Blochberger (advised by Takeru Igusa, PhD)

Johns Hopkins University, Department of Civil & Systems Engineering

A methodological three-layer computational framework coupling system dynamics, agent-based modeling, and an evolving social network to model HRQoL dynamics in AYA cancer patients. The novel research contribution is the formally specified, verified, bidirectionally coupled architecture of six switchable interfaces with applications to AYA cancer research.
